COLORBEE BETA — START HERE

Thanks for trying Colorbee. It's a Mac paint and editing app: the quickness of
classic MS Paint, plus layers, effects, annotation and redaction for screenshots.
This is a beta, so expect a few rough edges. Telling Leah about them is the
whole point.


WHAT YOU NEED

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)
- macOS 26 or later


INSTALLING

1. Drag Colorbee.app into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. macOS may say it was downloaded from the internet; click Open.
   Colorbee is signed and checked by Apple, so you won't need to change any
   security settings.


GETTING AROUND

- Tools are in the toolbar. Most have a one-key shortcut, shown when you
  hover over them (P Pencil, B Brush, E Eraser, G Fill, T Text, M Select...).
- Left-click paints with Color 1, right-click with Color 2, as in Paint.
- Click a swatch to set the ringed color well; right-click sets Color 2.
- Hover over almost anything to see what it does.
- Every change can be undone (⌘Z), with no step limit. The History panel
  (⌘Y) lets you jump back to any step.
- Layers: ⌘L shows the Layers panel.
- Settings (⌘,) has your keyboard shortcuts and export sizes.


A FEW THINGS WORTH TRYING

- Take a screenshot to the clipboard (⇧⌃⌘4), then File ▸ Paste into New Image.
- Effects ▸ Auto-Redact... finds email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers
  and keys in a screenshot so you can hide them.
- View ▸ Before/After compares your edits with the original.
- File ▸ Export As picks a common size for sharing.


SAVING

- Plain images save as PNG (or whatever format you opened).
- Once an image has more than one layer, it saves as a Colorbee project
  (.colorproj) so the layers are kept. Use File ▸ Export... for a flat copy.
- Colorbee saves automatically, like Pages and TextEdit.


PRIVACY

Colorbee has no internet access at all. Nothing you open, draw or redact
leaves your Mac. Auto-Redact reads text on your Mac using Apple's built-in
text recognition.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

- Tell Leah what you were doing, what you expected and what happened.
  A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
- If Colorbee quits unexpectedly, macOS shows a report. Click "Report...",
  then copy the text in the details and send it to Leah. It holds technical
  details about where the app stopped, not your pictures or files.
