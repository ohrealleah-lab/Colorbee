Colorbee Help

Annotate an image

Arrows, boxes, numbered steps and labels: everything you need to point at something in a screenshot and send it on.

Arrows and shapes

  1. Click Shapes in the toolbar (or press U) and choose a shape from the gallery: Arrow, Line, Rectangle, a callout and 20 more.
  2. Drag on the image. Hold Shift for a square, a circle, or a line at 45° steps.
  3. The shape stays editable: drag it to move it, drag its handles to resize or rotate it, and change its outline, fill or colors in the toolbar and palette bar.
  4. Click outside it, press Return or switch tools to place it. Esc throws it away.
  • Colors: a left-drag outlines with Color 1 and fills with Color 2. A right-drag swaps them.
  • Outline and Fill can each be None, Solid, or a textured style: Crayon, Marker, Oil, Watercolor or Natural Pencil.
  • Curve: drag the line, then click or drag twice to bend it.
  • Polygon: click each corner. Click the first corner again, or double-click, to close it.

Numbered steps

The Step Badge tool (N) places numbered circles, for "click here first, then here".

  1. Press N, then click where step 1 goes. The next click places 2, then 3, and so on.
  2. To point at something, drag instead of clicking: the badge goes where you press, with an arrow to where you let go.
  • The circle is Color 1 with the number in Color 2. Right-click swaps them.
  • In the palette bar, choose 1, 2, 3 or A, B, C. Numbers and letters count separately. Next shows, and lets you change, what the next badge will say.
  • Size sets the circle's width.
  • Until you place it, a badge can be dragged to a new spot, or removed with Delete. Undoing a badge steps the count back.

Text

  1. Press T, then click where the text goes, or drag to set how wide it is.
  2. Type. The palette bar has the font, size, Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough and alignment.
  3. Background: Opaque puts a Color 2 box behind the text, which reads well over a busy screenshot.
  4. Click outside the text, press Esc or switch tools to place it.

Once placed, text becomes pixels and can't be edited again, as in classic Paint. Check the spelling before you click away.

Text styles: set up a look you like, then choose Styles ▸ Save Current Style… in the palette bar. Pick it from Styles next time. Styles are kept between launches.

Highlight with the Marker

Choose Brushes ▸ Marker and a yellow Color 1, then paint over the text. The Marker is see-through, and going over the same spot in one stroke doesn't make it darker, so it works like a highlighter pen. Hold Shift as you drag to keep the stroke straight along a line of text.

Crop and size it

  • Crop to Selection (⇧⌘X): select the part to keep, then crop.
  • Image ▸ Crop… gives you a crop box with set shapes (Square, 16:9 and others) and exact pixel sizes. Guides show while you drag the box; O switches between them (Thirds, Grid, Diagonal, Triangle, Golden Ratio, Golden Spiral). Return applies, Esc cancels.
  • File ▸ Export As saves a PNG at a common width, such as Slack (760 px). It keeps the shape and never makes an image bigger.

Resize and Skew

Image ▸ Resize and Skew… (⌘E) makes the selection bigger or smaller, or the whole image if nothing is selected.

  • Percentage (1–500%) or exact Pixels. Maintain aspect ratio keeps the shape.
  • Smooth (photos) for photos and screenshots; Sharp (pixel art) keeps each pixel a crisp block.
  • Skew slants it sideways (Horizontal) or up and down (Vertical), from −89° to 89°.
  • The size it will be shows at the bottom before you click OK.

Canvas Properties

Image ▸ Canvas Properties… (⌥⌘E) changes the size of the canvas without stretching the picture: it's like changing the size of the paper.

  • The picture stays at the top-left. A bigger canvas adds space on the right and bottom, filled with Color 2; a smaller one trims the right and bottom.
  • Transparent background makes new space, and what the Eraser leaves behind, see-through instead of Color 2. The pixels already there don't change.
  • You can also drag the handles on the canvas's right edge, bottom edge or corner.

Send it

  • Copy (⌘C) with nothing selected copies the whole image, ready to paste into Slack, Mail or Messages.
  • File ▸ Share… sends it with AirDrop, Messages, Mail and more.

Copy the text out of a picture

Edit ▸ Copy Text (⌥⌘C) reads the words in the selection, or the whole image, and puts them on the clipboard as plain text. It reads on your Mac, and never changes the image. A short message says how many words it copied.
