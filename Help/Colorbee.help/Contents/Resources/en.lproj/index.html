Colorbee Help

Colorbee is a Mac paint and editing app: the quickness of classic Paint, plus layers, effects, annotation and redaction for screenshots, PDFs and photos.

Guides

  • Getting started: a tour of the window, colors, keys and undo.
  • Redact a screenshot: hide emails, phone numbers and keys before you share.
  • Annotate an image: arrows, shapes, numbered steps, text and highlights.
  • Work with layers: keep parts separate, and change their look with adjustment layers.
  • Fix up a photo: light and color, straightening, removing things, red-eye and RAW photos.
  • Pages and PDFs: edit every page of a PDF, scan from your iPhone, and make PDFs.
  • Pixel art: the Pencil, the pixel grid, symmetry and the Color Eraser.
  • Save, export and share: formats, projects, export sizes and earlier versions.

Reference

  • Tools: every tool, its settings, and its keys.
  • Menus: every menu command and what it does.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: every key, all in one place.
  • Panels: Layers, Adjustments, History, Clipboard History, pages and Before/After.
  • Settings: appearance, PDF resolution, shortcuts and export presets.
  • Privacy: what stays on your Mac, and what goes into the files you share.
  • Troubleshooting: common surprises, and how to report a problem.

You can also hover over almost anything in Colorbee to see what it does.
