Colorbee Help

Panels

The sidebar on the right holds five panels. Show each from the View menu, or with the buttons at the top of the sidebar. Turning off the last one closes the sidebar.

To hide the whole sidebar for more room, press ⇧S, choose View ▸ Sidebar (⌃⌘S), or click the Sidebar button in the toolbar. Do it again and the same panels come back. While Adjust Photo is open its sliders are in the sidebar, so it stays.

Layers (⌘L)

The layers of the page you're looking at, top layer first. Click one to make it active, double-click to rename it, drag to reorder. Each has an eye (show or hide) and a padlock (lock). Along the top: the blend mode, the opacity and, for an adjustment layer, Only the layer below. Along the bottom: New, Duplicate, Delete, Merge Down and the Adjustment ▾ menu. See Work with layers.

Adjustments

The settings of the active adjustment layer, with a live preview. Reset puts them back; Merge makes the adjustment permanent (Layer ▸ Merge Adjustment). Some, like Levels, also have Auto. Drag a slider's knob, or drag sideways anywhere on its name line.

History (⌘Y)

Every step you've taken on this page, each with a name and a small picture. Click a step to go back to it. The steps after it are dimmed, and you can go forward to them again until you make a new change. In a document with pages, it shows the page you're looking at.

Clipboard History (⌥⌘V)

  • The last 10 images you copied or pasted, kept between launches.
  • Click one to paste it as a selection you can move. The Mac's clipboard isn't changed.
  • Hover over one to see when it was copied and its size. Right-click to remove one; Clear forgets them all.

Info (⌥⌘I)

Facts about the page you're looking at. You can read them and copy them, but not change them.

  • File: its name, its kind (such as PNG image) and its size on disk, or "Not saved yet".
  • Image: its size in pixels, its color profile, whether the background is transparent, and how many layers it has. A document with pages also shows which page this is; a page from a PDF shows the resolution it was opened at.
  • Photo: for photos from a camera or phone, the camera, lens, ISO, shutter speed, aperture, focal length and date taken. When the original file had a location, it says Location: Removed: Colorbee never keeps it.

Page sidebar (⌥⌘2)

Down the left side, once a document has more than one page. Click a page to edit it, drag to reorder, right-click for New, Duplicate and Delete Page. See Pages and PDFs.

Before/After (⌥⌘B)

View ▸ Before/After compares the image now with how it was. It's for looking only, and never changes the image.

  • Edited against: As Opened (when you opened it, or made it from a paste), or Last Saved (once it's been saved).
  • Side by side, or Split with a divider you drag across.
