Colorbee Help

Privacy

Colorbee has no internet access at all. Nothing you open, draw, redact or read leaves your Mac.

What's done on your Mac

These use Apple's built-in Vision, which runs on your Mac:

  • Auto-Redact and Edit ▸ Copy Text read the text in your image.
  • Select Subject, Remove Background and Lift Subject find the subject of a photo.
  • Remove Red-Eye finds the eyes.

Colorbee has no AI models of its own. Retouch tools such as Remove work only from the pixels already in your image.

Copies a redaction can leave behind

  • Earlier versions and undo: after you redact and save with ⌘S, if the file has earlier versions, Colorbee offers Remove Earlier Versions and Undo History, so the original can't be brought back.
  • Clipboard History and Copy Text: if the unredacted image is in Clipboard History, or text you copied with Copy Text is still on the clipboard, Colorbee offers to remove it, and to clear the clipboard if it still holds the image.
  • Blur and Pixelate can sometimes be read back. Solid Fill can't.

See Redact a screenshot.

What goes into the files you share

  • Export as PDF writes pixels only: no hidden text under a redaction, and no title, author, app name or dates.
  • Camera details (camera, lens, exposure and the date taken) go into an exported photo only if you turn on Include camera details in File ▸ Export…. A Colorbee project keeps them, so share an export rather than the project. Colorbee never keeps or writes the location, serial numbers or the owner's name.

Files Colorbee keeps

In your Library ▸ Application Support ▸ Colorbee folder:

  • Clipboard History: the last 10 images you copied or pasted. Clear in the Clipboard History panel removes them.
  • Desktop Pictures: the picture you set with File ▸ Set as Desktop Picture. Only the one in use is kept.
  • Filters.json: the photo filters you've saved.

Copies made for File ▸ Share… are temporary, and removed the next time you share or open Colorbee.

An untitled image is autosaved by macOS until you save or close it, so it comes back after a crash.
