Colorbee Help

Tools

Every tool in the toolbar, in order. A tool's settings appear in the palette bar when you choose it; Size, and Outline and Fill, are in the toolbar. Keys shown are the defaults; see Keyboard shortcuts.

For most tools, a left-click uses Color 1 and a right-click uses Color 2. Tools work on the active layer, except the Redact Brush, which changes every layer.

Selection tools

Rectangle Select (M), Ellipse Select and Free-Form Select (L)

  • Drag to select. Free-Form closes itself when you let go.
  • Hold a key as you start dragging outside the selection: Shift adds to the selection, Option takes away from it, and Shift-Option keeps only the overlap.
  • Press Shift after you've started dragging to make a square or a circle.
  • Feather in the palette bar softens the edge of each new selection, from 0 (hard) to 100 px.

Magic Wand (W)

  • Click to select an area of similar color. Shift, Option and Shift-Option combine selections as above.
  • Tolerance sets how similar a color must be. Contiguous selects only the connected area; off, it selects every matching pixel. Sample All Layers looks at all visible layers together.

Working with a selection

  • Drag inside it to move the pixels. Option-drag moves a copy. Shift-drag leaves a trail of copies, the classic Paint trick.
  • Drag a handle to stretch it; hold Shift to keep its proportions. Resize in the palette bar chooses Smooth or Sharp pixels.
  • Arrow keys nudge moved pixels 1 pixel, or 10 with Shift. ⌘ and an arrow grows or shrinks the selection itself.
  • Edit ▸ Feather… softens the selection's edge. Edit ▸ Modify Selection grows it evenly all round (Expand…), shrinks it (Contract…), or rounds off ragged edges, fills small gaps and removes stray specks (Smooth…), by 1–100 px. While you choose, a red tint shows what isn't selected. A soft edge stays soft, and the canvas's edge stays selected. Like drawing a selection, these aren't undo steps.
  • Click outside it, press Return or switch tools to put the pixels down.
  • Edit ▸ Color Range… selects by color: click a color in the picture, and Shift-click to add more. Fuzziness takes in colors further from those you clicked; the closest are fully selected and the rest fade out, so the edge is soft. Edit ▸ Luminance Range… selects by brightness: drag the two handles, or choose Shadows, Midtones or Highlights (the one in use is highlighted), and Smoothness sets how gently it fades beyond them. In both, a red tint shows what isn't selected, Selection chooses whether the result replaces, adds to, takes away from or overlaps the selection you have, and Sample All Layers reads every visible layer, as the Magic Wand's does. Press Return to select, or Esc to cancel. Adjust Photo and the other effects then change the selected parts, fading across the soft edge.
  • Edit ▸ Graduated Selection selects with a fade, like Lightroom's linear gradient: drag a line from where it should be fully selected to where it should fade to nothing. Drag either end to move it, or drag somewhere else to start again; Shift keeps the line straight across, down or at 45°. Selection in the bar combines it with the selection you have. Press Return to select. Then Adjust Photo darkens a sky, say, most at the top and less and less toward the horizon.
  • Edit ▸ Paste in Place (⌥⇧⌘V) pastes copied pixels exactly where you copied them from, in the same image or another one, instead of at the top-left of the view. A picture copied in another app has no place, so it pastes as ⌘V would; so does one that wouldn't land on the canvas.

Transparent Selection

The button after the Magic Wand. When it's on, pixels matching Color 2 are left out when you move or paste, so a white background doesn't come along.

Pencil (P)

Hard one-pixel lines, for pixel art. Shift keeps the line straight across or up and down. It ignores pen pressure.

Brushes (B)

Choose from the gallery: Round, Calligraphy 1 and 2, Airbrush, Oil, Crayon, Marker, Natural Pencil and Watercolor.

  • Size 1–50 px. Every brush follows the Alpha slider.
  • On a Force Touch trackpad or a pen tablet, pressing harder makes the stroke bigger. Turn this off with Pressure changes the size, at the bottom of the gallery.
  • Shift keeps the stroke straight across or up and down.
  • Going over the same spot in one stroke doesn't make it darker. The Marker is see-through, like a highlighter.

Retouch (J)

Remove, Spot Heal, Clone Stamp (S), Lighten, Darken, Saturate, Desaturate, Blur, Sharpen and Smudge. Size 1–300 px. See Fix up a photo for what each one is for.

  • Strength and Hardness for the brushes; Range (Shadows, Midtones, Highlights) for Lighten and Darken.
  • Aligned and Match Tone for the Clone Stamp. Option-click sets where it copies from.
  • Sample All Layers for Remove, Spot Heal and the Clone Stamp, so you can retouch onto an empty layer.
  • Right-click does the same as left-click. Each stroke is one step to undo.

Shapes (U)

Choose from the gallery: Arrow, Line, Curve, Rectangle, Rounded Rectangle, Ellipse, triangles, Diamond, Pentagon, Hexagon, block arrows, stars, three callouts, Heart, Lightning and Polygon.

  • Drag to draw. Shift keeps proportions: squares, circles, and lines at 45° steps.
  • Left-drag outlines with Color 1 and fills with Color 2; right-drag swaps them.
  • Outline and Fill in the toolbar: None, Solid, or a textured style. The Size sets the outline's width.
  • The shape stays editable (move, resize, rotate, restyle) until you click outside it or press Return. Esc throws it away.

Fill (G)

Fills the connected area of similar color. Tolerance sets how similar; Sample All Layers finds the area in everything you see but fills on the active layer. It stays inside a selection.

Text (T)

Click to type, or drag to set the width. The palette bar has the font, size, Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, alignment, Background (Transparent or an Opaque Color 2 box) and saved Styles. Text becomes pixels when you click outside it or press Esc.

Eraser (E)

  • A square eraser. A left-drag paints Color 2, or clear on a transparent layer. Shift keeps it straight.
  • A right-drag is the Color Eraser: it changes only Color 1 pixels to Color 2. Turn on Color Eraser in the palette bar to do that with a left-drag. Tolerance sets how close a match must be.

Eyedropper (I)

Click to set Color 1, right-click for Color 2. Option-click picks from all visible layers together. Sample Size picks one pixel (1 px), or the average of the 3 × 3 or 5 × 5 pixels around the click: handy on a photo's noise or a letter's soft edge. Add Color 1 to Custom Colors keeps it in the palette bar.

Magnifier (Z)

Click to zoom in, right-click or Option-click to zoom out.

Gradient

Drag to blend from Color 1 to Color 2; right-drag reverses it. Choose Linear, Radial, Reflected, Diamond or Conical. A see-through color fades to clear. It stays inside a selection.

Measure (R)

Drag between two points. The status bar shows the distance in pixels, the width and height, and the angle. The line is never drawn into the image.

Redact Brush (K)

Paint over anything to hide it with Solid Fill, Blur or Pixelate, applied when you let go. Size 1–300 px. Shift keeps it straight along a line of text. It changes every layer with pixels under the stroke. See Redact a screenshot.

Step Badge (N)

Click to place numbered circles, 1, 2, 3 or A, B, C. Drag to add an arrow. Size 16–128 px. See Annotate an image.

Size

In the toolbar, for the tools that have a size: five preset sizes and a box for any size. [ and ] make it smaller or larger.
