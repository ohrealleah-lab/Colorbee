Colorbee Help

Troubleshooting

Common surprises, and what's going on.

My image saved as a project, not a PNG

Once an image has more than one layer, an adjustment layer or more than one page, it saves as a Colorbee project, so nothing is flattened away. Use File ▸ Export… for a PNG or JPEG copy. See Save, export and share.

A menu command is greyed out

  • The layer is locked. Click its padlock in the Layers panel to unlock it.
  • An adjustment layer is active. Painting and Merge Down need a pixel layer. Click a pixel layer first.
  • It needs a selection, such as Crop to Selection, Spotlight… or Redact ▸ Solid Fill with Color 1. Select an area first.
  • A dialog or effect is still open. Finish or cancel it first.

I can't draw: the pointer shows a "not allowed" sign

The active layer is locked, or it's an adjustment layer. Unlock it, or click a pixel layer in the Layers panel.

⌘Z didn't undo my selection

Drawing a selection on its own isn't a step, so ⌘Z undoes the last change to the image instead. To take part of a selection away, hold Option and start dragging outside the selection, across the part to remove, or Option-click with the Magic Wand. (Option-dragging from inside the selection moves a copy of it.) ⌘D removes the whole selection.

Remove left a smear

Remove fills from the area around it, which works best on simple backgrounds such as sky, walls and sand. In busy spots, where edges and textures meet, undo it and use the Clone Stamp (S) instead: Option-click a clean area, then paint.

"No text found", "No subject found" or "No eyes found"

  • No text found (Copy Text): the text may be too small or blurry for your Mac to read. A sharper, larger image helps.
  • No subject found: screenshots and documents have no subject. It works on photos of people, pets and things.
  • No sensitive text found (Auto-Redact): Colorbee read the text but found nothing it looks for. Add your own pattern under Patterns, or redact by hand.
  • No eyes found or No red-eye found: select the red pupils, then choose Remove Red-Eye again.

A RAW photo won't open

Colorbee reads the RAW files macOS can read. A camera newer than your version of macOS may not be supported yet; updating macOS usually adds it. In the meantime, open a JPEG or HEIC from the camera.

Reporting a problem

  • Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
  • If Colorbee quits unexpectedly, macOS shows a report. Click Report…, then copy the details and send them. They describe where the app stopped, not your pictures or files.
